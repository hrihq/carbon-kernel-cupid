Engineered to Perfection

Every subsystem tuned to address real Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 pain points without sacrificing system stability.

⚡

Anti-2W Charge Floor

Prevents Qualcomm charging thermal throttling from dropping charging current to a 500mA (2W) trickle. Sustains fast charging reliably between 18W–33W even under warm device temperatures.

❄️

WALT Scroll Cooling

Boost util raised to 100 and colocation to 70. Light scrolling threads stay pinned to the efficient Cortex-A510 Little cluster. The hot Cortex-X2 Prime core sleeps until heavy games demand it.

🛡️

KernelSU-Next + SUSFS

Integrated KernelSU-Next v3.4.2 paired with latest SUSFS v2.3.0 for transparent root cloaking. Keeps banking apps, Google Pay, and Play Integrity checks running without issues.

🎛️

Dual-Build Recovery Selector

Single universal flashable AnyKernel3 zip with volume key switcher: Vol UP for Root (KernelSU + SUSFS), Vol DOWN for 100% Stock Vanilla + auto wipe /data/adb.

🌐

TCP BBR & FQ Network

Google BBR congestion control algorithm with Fair Queueing packet scheduling out-of-the-box for low bufferbloat and ultra-stable multiplayer gaming ping.

🎯

Exact Vendor Module ABI

Built with Google Android Clang r596125 (Clang 22.0.2) and Full LTO. 100% symbol CRC match with official AOSP A16/A17 vendor display and touch drivers.
