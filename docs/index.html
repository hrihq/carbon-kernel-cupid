Architectural Precision

Targeted kernel modifications addressing known Qualcomm Waipio bottlenecks.

Power Supply

Anti-2W Hardware Floor

Clamps minimum FCC and ICL in xm_charger_votable.c and step-chg-jeita.c to 2500mA. Eliminates the 500mA (2W) trickle collapse at 43C battery temperature.

WALT Scheduler

Scroll Cooling Engine

Boost util raised to 100 and colocation to 70. Light scrolling threads stay pinned to the efficient Cortex-A510 Little cluster. The hot Cortex-X2 Prime core sleeps.

Root Security

KernelSU-Next & SUSFS

Integrated KernelSU-Next v3.4.2 paired with latest SUSFS v2.3.0 for transparent root cloaking. Keeps banking apps and Play Integrity checks clean.

Installer

Dual-Build Recovery Selector

Single universal flashable AnyKernel3 zip with volume key switcher: Vol UP for Root (KernelSU + SUSFS), Vol DOWN for Vanilla Clean Stock.

Networking

TCP BBR & FQ Queueing

Google BBR congestion control algorithm with Fair Queueing packet scheduling out-of-the-box for low bufferbloat and steady online gaming ping.

Toolchain

Exact Vendor Module ABI

Built with Google Android Clang r596125 (Clang 22.0.2) and Full LTO. 100% symbol CRC match with official AOSP A16/A17 vendor display and touch drivers.
